The presentation mark covers five slides plus the Q&A. The five slides map to formulation, design, analysis and conclusions. The statement of contributions is required for any credit on the presentation upload and the Q&A.

A proposal grade of 5 out of 10 or less, or an incomplete grade, signals a major rethink and may lead to a resubmission. A resubmission is marked with a maximum of 8 out of 10. Nothing in the marked copy indicates that this applies to the team.
